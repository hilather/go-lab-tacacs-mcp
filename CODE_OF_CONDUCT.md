# Code of conduct

TacLab contributors are expected to treat each other professionally.

- Be respectful in reviews, issues, and discussion.
- Assume good faith; criticize code and designs, not people.
- Do not harass, insult, or exclude others.
- Do not share secrets, private keys, or personal data in public channels.

Project maintainers may refuse, edit, or revert contributions that violate this standard, and may block people who persist after a warning.

Reports may be sent through the private security contact in [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/hilather/go-lab-tacacs-mcp/blob/main/SECURITY.md) when a public issue would cause harm.
